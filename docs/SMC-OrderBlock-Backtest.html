SMC Order Block Backtest

HTF Order Block retest → LTF CISD confirmation, tested on MNQ, MES, MYM, MGC from 2021-09-01 to 2026-10-03: five years of 1-minute data covering the 2022 bear market, the 2023–24 rally and the 2025–26 tape. The brief: a profit factor above 1, net profit at least 3× the maximum drawdown, and within that, the highest win rate. 2 of 4 markets have a set, picked on in-sample data alone, that meets the brief over five years and stays profitable out-of-sample.

What the numbers say

MNQMeets the brief and holds out-of-sample. NT8: 61 trades, 60.7% wins, +$9,717, max DD $1,651 (net/DD 5.9). Profitable in every calendar year, 2022 included.
MESMeets the brief on paper (net/DD 4.3, 60.5% wins), but the dollars are small (+$2,600 in five years) and 2025 lost $370. The walk-forward came out flat.
MYMJust short: net/DD 2.98 in NT8 (54.5% wins, +$2,127 on 222 trades). Profitable every year, in small amounts.
MGCHighest win rate (77.6%, 0.5R target) and the best walk-forward, but one $1,304 drawdown in 2026 pulls five-year net/DD down to 2.1.
  • The defaults don't make money. Over five years at 1 contract, the strategy as it ships loses $3,336 on MNQ and $3,325 on MYM, breaks even on MES, and makes $3,377 on MGC (net/DD 1.4). Every recommended set beats its defaults.
  • Where the edge is. Win rate and the 3:1 ratio come from small targets (0.5–1R) on higher-timeframe blocks (H4 Order Blocks with M15 CISD on MNQ and MGC, H1 with M5 on MES), with the trend filter off and, on the index micros, long only. The bearish-block side of the index sets lost money in most of the grid.
  • Regime check. The 2021–26 window is mostly an index bull market, which flatters long-only sets. The MNQ pick still made +$2,876 in the 2022 bear market (15 trades), the best sign that its edge is not just the trend. MES and MYM made far less in 2022.
  • Forward test. Re-optimising every six months and trading the next six untouched (the walk-forward) gave: MNQ +$1,102 (net/DD 0.4), MGC +$1,501 (1.4), MES −$110, MYM −$427. The forward-tested ratios are far below the in-sample ones. Expect the live ratio to land well under 3:1.
  • Monte Carlo (MNQ pick). Over five years, 2% of resampled histories lose money; the median max drawdown is $1,937 and the 95th percentile $3,810. In any single year there is a 19% chance of a losing year and a 5% chance of a drawdown above $2,123 (1 contract).
  • Frequency. The picks trade 12–16 times a year (MYM about 44). That's too few to judge from a short live sample. Give any of them several months on sim first.

Recommendation: SMC_MNQ_Rec is the one set that meets the brief, survives out-of-sample, stays positive in the walk-forward and was confirmed by NT8. Run it on sim or with the smallest live size first. Treat MES as a secondary candidate. Don't deploy the MYM or MGC picks on this evidence alone.

How it was tested

Data

NinjaTrader 8 downloaded 1-minute history for every contract from mid-2021 through the current front month (Rithmic feed). A small dump strategy (SMCBarDump) then wrote out the exact 1-minute series NT8 feeds a Strategy Analyzer run: the merged, back-adjusted continuous contract, rolled on NT8's own rollover dates. The replica reads that file, so both see identical bars. Trading starts 2021-09-01; the three months before it only warm up the detectors.

  • MNQ: Micro E-mini Nasdaq-100, 1,891,434 one-minute bars 2021-05-31 to 2026-10-02 across 22 contracts (front month now MNQ 12-26)
  • MES: Micro E-mini S&P 500, 1,881,102 one-minute bars 2021-05-31 to 2026-10-02 across 22 contracts (front month now MES 12-26)
  • MYM: Micro E-mini Dow, 1,842,428 one-minute bars 2021-05-31 to 2026-10-02 across 22 contracts (front month now MYM 12-26)
  • MGC: Micro Gold, 1,865,014 one-minute bars 2021-05-31 to 2026-10-02 across 26 contracts (front month now MGC 12-26)

Replica, and how closely it matches NinjaTrader

A NinjaTrader run of five years of 1-minute data across four series takes minutes, too slow for thousands of combinations. The sweep uses a replica instead: SMCOrderBlockStrategy.cs and SMCZobEngines.cs compiled unchanged against a small stand-in for the NinjaTrader API, fed the same four series in NT8's order (bars that close at the same moment run in series order) and filled with the Strategy Analyzer's historical rules: market orders at the next 1-minute open, the stop and target live from the fill bar, stop first when one bar touches both, targets only on trade-through and at their limit price, and every series' fills for a minute settled before any series updates on that minute. Sweep

  1. Stage A, structure grid (1,296 sets per market): timeframe pair (H4→M15, H1→M5, M15→M1) × target 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, 3 R × trend filter on/off × FVG off / confluence / required × CISD liquidity sweep on/off × single or multi CISD and retest × direction (both, long only, short only).
  2. Stage B, refinement: the six best stage-A sets (at most two per timeframe pair) had every secondary input varied one at a time: displacement, swing strength, relative strength, trend gate and length, mitigation and invalidation rules, CISD level and series length, CISD search window, OB expiry, opposite-block exit, ride-the-trend, break-even (three trigger distances) and the full target ladder. Each base moved to its best variant, and the round repeated (up to three rounds). Scored on in-sample data only.
  3. Selection rule: on in-sample data (2021-09-01 to 2025-01-01), keep sets with a profit factor above 1, net profit ≥ 3× max drawdown, at least 12 trades a year and 10 losing trades; pick the highest win rate (ties: higher net/DD). The out-of-sample years (2025-01-01 to 2026-10-03) are never used to choose.
  4. Walk-forward: the same rule re-run at 2023-07, 2024-01, 2024-07, 2025-01, 2025-07, 2026-01, 2026-07, each pick trading the following six months.
  5. Monte Carlo: 5,000 day-block bootstraps and 5,000 trade-order shuffles of the recommended set.
  6. NinjaTrader confirmation: each market's recommended set runs in NT8's Strategy Analyzer over the full five years with commission.

Know before you trade it

  • Trades are rare. One position at a time, and a setup needs a fresh higher-timeframe Order Block, a retest, and a CISD in the trend's direction. Expect weeks without a trade; small samples make every ratio noisy.
  • Win-rate selection pulls the target in. The highest win rates come with targets below 1R. Such a set needs its win rate to hold: a few points lower and the edge is gone. The Monte Carlo and walk-forward sections show how much room there is.
  • No slippage modelled. Market entries fill at the next 1-minute open and stops at their price. On the micros a tick of slippage per side costs $0.50 (MNQ, MYM), $1.25 (MES) or $1 (MGC) per contract per side.
  • Back-adjusted prices. Older contracts are shifted to line up with the current one, so 2021 prices in the trade lists are not the prices traded then. P&L in points is unaffected.
  • In-sample choices. Thousands of sets were tried; the in-sample winner is optimistic by construction. The out-of-sample columns and the walk-forward are the fair estimate.
  • Walk-forward pool. At each cut-off the rule re-picks from every set the sweep tried, and the refinement that added some of those sets was steered by data through 2024. Cut-offs before 2025 therefore choose from a slightly better-informed pool than a trader would have had; read the walk-forward as a ceiling, not a floor.
  • Live differences. NT8 live checks break-even and ride-the-trend on bar close of the 1-minute series, as in this backtest; the MT5 EA checked every tick.
